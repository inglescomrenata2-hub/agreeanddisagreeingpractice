Key language

Agreeing: "I feel the same."  ·  "I think so, too."  ·  "Exactly!"

Disagreeing politely: "Well, Lara sees it differently."  ·  "We understand your idea, but we're not so sure."

Past simple → a finished action. Someone suggested a uniform. I said, "Exactly!"

Past continuous (was / were + -ing) → an action in progress at that moment. I was writing an email when she suggested it.

while + past continuous · when + past simple: While we were talking, the manager came in.

Useful verbs: suggest → suggested · agree → agreed · think → thought · say → said · feel → felt · believe → believed · understand → understood

The situation: Last week, there was a team meeting at your school office. People suggested these ideas:
  • Give employees training on how to use smart technology.
  • Paint the walls a different colour.
  • Let employees work from home.
  • Everyone wears the same uniform.
  • Make lunch breaks shorter.

Many answers are possible. Examples:
